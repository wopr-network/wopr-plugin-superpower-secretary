# Fire Your Secretary

She never calls in sick.

---

Your $5 chief of staff reads your email before you do, manages your calendar, and reminds you of deadlines before they slip. She doesn't wait to be asked. She just handles it.

Every morning, she delivers a briefing: today's schedule, pending emails that need replies, and anything that's about to be late. You don't have to open your inbox. She already triaged it.

This isn't a tool. It's a person on your team who happens to cost less than your coffee.

## What it can do

- Read your inbox, summarize threads, draft replies
- Manage your calendar: suggest time blocks, flag conflicts, remind you what's next
- Track deadlines and send reminders before things slip
- Deliver a daily morning briefing without being asked
- Coordinate across email and calendar so nothing falls through the cracks

## What gets installed

- **Cron** -- scheduled tasks (morning briefing, deadline checks)
- **Gmail** -- email reading, drafting, sending
- **Calendar** -- schedule management and conflict detection
